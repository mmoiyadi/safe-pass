Two directions, on the current build

Both keep every capability the app has today — multiple vaults, template-driven fields, custom fields, folder and tag filing, sharing with key rotation, offline read-only sessions, six account settings panels. They differ in where structure lives: 2a keeps it permanently on screen, 2b hides it behind search and opens things over the list.

2a

Workbench

One rail holds the whole vault hierarchy, so vault, folder and tag are always answerable at a glance. The list becomes one line per secret; fields live in a detail pane instead of being dumped into every row. Settings gets its own panel index rather than one long scroll.

Your master password is used here, in this browser, and never sent. The server holds ciphertext it cannot open — so there is no recovery path.

Change master password

Only your key is re-wrapped — none of your secrets are re-encrypted, so this is fast however large your vault is. Any backup you exported earlier still opens only with the password that was in force when you took it.

Your other signed-in devices will ask for the new password the next time they do anything. A device that is offline right now keeps access to its cached copy until it reconnects.

Strong — acceptable
At least 12 characters. A passphrase of several unrelated words is both stronger and easier to remember than a short, complicated one.
Enabled once the new password is acceptable.
There is no password reset to fall back on. If you forget this password, nobody — including us — can open the vault.
